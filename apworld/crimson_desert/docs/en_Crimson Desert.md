# Crimson Desert

## What does randomization do to this game?

Selected quests, boss victories, discoveries and treasures become Archipelago checks. Completing one sends an item
to someone in the multiworld. Progression unlocks that would normally come from those activities are shuffled into
the multiworld, so other players can find them for you.

## Current status

**Pre-alpha, first slice.** The world covers the prologue and chapter 1: 42 checks, each an objective from the
quest log (for example "In Ashes: Help Naira") or an Abyss puzzle. An objective's check also counts once its
mission is complete, so skipping one never loses it.

Items: **Flight**, which the story would normally give you at the woman in white (install the
`flight-grant-removed` override with `crimson-mod` so it doesn't); three **Abyss Artifacts**, to spend on Flight
upgrades; and filler: **100 Copper**, or now and then a Crow Feather, which does nothing in the game.

## What is the goal?

Speak with the woman in white (the end of the first slice). Longer goals come with more of the game.

## What does another world's item look like in Crimson Desert?

Checks are reported by the Crimsonipelago client; the client's activity feed shows what you sent and received.
In-game notifications are planned but not part of the first release.
